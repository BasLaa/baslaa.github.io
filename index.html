About

I am a PhD student at the Laboratory for Foundations of Computer Science (LFCS) at the University of Edinburgh, supervised by Ohad Kammar .

My research interests are in programming languages, type theory, category theory, and the semantics of computation.

Research

I am broadly interested in mathematical and categorical approaches to the theory of programming languages.

Current interests include:

  • Type theory and semantics
  • Termination and productivity
  • Sized and guarded type systems
  • Category-theoretic semantics of programming languages
  • Inductive and coinductive types
