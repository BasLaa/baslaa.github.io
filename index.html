About

I am a PhD student at the Laboratory for Foundations of Computer Science (LFCS) at the University of Edinburgh, supervised by Ohad Kammar. I previously did the Master of Logic at the ILLC, University of Amsterdam. My MSc thesis was supervised by Benno van den Berg and Daniël Otten.

My research interests are in logic, type theory, category theory, and programming languages.
